### What Drives Used-Car Prices?

Used‑car prices are shaped by a handful of consistent, intuitive factors. After cleaning and analyzing the dataset Kurt provided, the strongest drivers of price were:

* **Year (Age)**: Newer cars are worth more. Prices decline steadily as vehicles age.
* **Mileage**: Lower mileage increases value. Cars with fewer miles command higher prices because they show less wear.
* **Vehicle Type**: Trucks and SUVs tend to sell for more, while sedans, coupes, and vans generally price lower.
* **Brand Tier**: Luxury brands (BMW, Mercedes, Lexus, Audi) carry a clear premium. Mid‑tier brands sit in the middle, and budget brands price lower.
* **Region**: Prices vary by geography. The West Coast and Northeast tend to be higher‑priced markets, while the South and Midwest trend lower.
* **Fuel Type**: Diesel and hybrid vehicles often price higher than gasoline models.
* **Engine Size (Cylinders)**: More cylinders generally mean a higher price, reflecting performance and vehicle class.
